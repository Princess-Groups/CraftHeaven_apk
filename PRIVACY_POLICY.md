# Privacy Policy — Athira's Creative Haven

*Last updated: August 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how **Athira's Creative Haven** ("we", "our", "us") — the craft store run by Athira — collects, uses, and protects information when you use our app or shop with us.

By using the app, you agree to this policy.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information** — When you sign up, we collect your email address and the name/phone you choose to provide. We use email + password for secure sign-in.

**Delivery & contact details** — When you place an order, we collect your name, phone number, and delivery address. We use these only to fulfil and deliver your order.

**Orders & shopping** — We store your cart, wishlist, orders, and order-tracking history so you can view and track them at any time.

**Payment information** — We do **not** collect or store your UPI, card, or bank details. Payments are completed inside your own UPI app (Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm / bank UPI). We only record that a payment was made (via the transaction reference you confirm) so we can mark your order as paid.

**Usage data** — Basic, non-identifying data needed to keep the app working (e.g., which pages you visit) may be collected for performance and stability.

## 2. How we use your information

- To create and manage your account
- To process and deliver your orders (including contacting you about them)
- To send you order status updates
- To improve our products and service
- To comply with legal and tax obligations (we are a registered small business and maintain sales records)

## 3. What we never do

- We never sell your personal information.
- We never share your details with advertisers.
- We never store your payment credentials.

## 4. Where your data lives

Your data is stored securely in a cloud database (Supabase) with password-based access, encrypted in transit. Only the store owner has access to admin/order records.

## 5. Push notifications (optional)

With your permission, we may send you order-status notifications. You can turn these off in your phone's app settings at any time.

## 6. Data retention

We keep order and billing records as required for tax and accounting purposes. You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us.

## 7. Children's privacy

Our app is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## 8. Your rights

You may:
- Access and update your account details in the app
- Request a copy of your data
- Request deletion of your account and data
- Withdraw consent for notifications

To exercise these, contact us at the email below.
